What I do

My present research focuses on the development of novel (adiabatic) pulses and pulse sequences for acquiring nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) spectra of strongly paramagnetic materials, and on devising new methods to fit, assign and understand paramagnetic NMR spectra. I study battery cathodes with NMR, electron paramagnetic resonance (EPR) and first-principles calculations. More about me

PublicationsPapers on paramagnetic NMR, EPR and battery materials. ToolsNMR frequencies, Curie-Weiss, CFSE, XRD and unit conversion. openNMRFree, open-source NMR processing, fitting and simulation. I maintain it.
